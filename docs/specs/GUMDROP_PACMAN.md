# Gumdrop Pac-Man

## Concept
A maze chaser where you collect snacks and avoid Zarg phantoms.

## Mechanics
- **Maze:** A top-down layout of Mildred's Citadel.
- **Collectibles:** Cheezies, chocolate bars, and gumdrops scattered throughout.
- **Enemies:** 4 Zarg phantoms patrol the maze.
- **Power-up:** Cream Soda cans allow Ben to temporarily stun the phantoms and walk through them.

## Engine Details
- `fizz-sim` handles maze navigation and grid-based movement.
