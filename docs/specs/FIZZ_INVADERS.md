# Fizz Invaders

## Concept
A Space Invaders clone where you play as Ben at the bottom of the screen, shooting fizz bubbles upward at waves of descending Zarg ships.

## Mechanics
- **Movement:** Left and right across the bottom of the screen.
- **Shooting:** Fire fizz bubbles straight up.
- **Enemies:** Formations of Zarg ships that move side-to-side and slowly descend. They occasionally drop bombs.
- **Defenses:** Chocolate blocks act as destructible shields between Ben and the Zargs.

## Engine Details
- Use `fizz-sim` for deterministic rules.
- View rendered via `fizz-slice-view`.
