# Pogo Panic

## Concept
A vertical platformer (like Doodle Jump) where Ben automatically pogos upwards on a series of procedurally generated platforms.

## Mechanics
- **Movement:** Ben pogos automatically; player controls left/right movement to land on platforms.
- **Hazards:** Crystal bats fly horizontally, Zarg phantoms hover, and some platforms crumble (chocolate blocks).
- **Goal:** Survive and climb as high as possible.

## Engine Details
- Utilize `fizz-phys` for Ben's pogo physics.
