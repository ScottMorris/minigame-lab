# Crystal Defender

## Concept
An Asteroids-style omnidirectional shooter. Ben is floating in the Crystal Caves, defending against incoming crystal bats and debris.

## Mechanics
- **Movement:** Rotate 360 degrees and thrust forward.
- **Shooting:** Fire fizz bubbles in the direction you are facing.
- **Enemies:** Crystal bats and debris that split into smaller pieces when shot.

## Engine Details
- `fizz-sim` for floating physics and collision detection.
